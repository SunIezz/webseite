⚠️ RESET is a non-commercial student project. These terms are provided for transparency. We are not a licensed medical provider. If you are in crisis, please call 112 immediately.

1. Acceptance of Terms

By accessing or using the RESET platform ("Service"), you agree to be bound by these Terms of Service. If you do not agree to these terms, please do not use the Service. You must be at least 18 years old to use RESET.

2. About RESET

RESET is a student-built project created by Team Return as part of an educational initiative. It is designed to provide peer support and information resources for young adults aged 18–25 who wish to reduce or stop alcohol consumption. RESET is not a licensed medical or psychological service.

3. User Accounts

When you create an account, you agree to:

  • Provide accurate and truthful information
  • Keep your password secure and confidential
  • Be responsible for all activity under your account
  • Notify us immediately of any unauthorized access

4. Community Rules

All users must follow our community guidelines:

  • Treat all members with respect and empathy
  • Do not share harmful or triggering content without a content warning
  • No promotion of alcohol, drugs, or self-harm
  • No harassment, hate speech, or discriminatory content
  • Do not share personal contact information of other users
  • Honor the privacy of private group conversations

5. Counseling Services

Free counseling sessions on RESET are conducted by volunteer psychology students and are not a substitute for professional licensed therapy, psychiatry, or emergency services. For emergencies, always call 112.

6. Payments

Certain features (Prevention Course at €20, Premium subscription at €4.99/month) require payment. All payments are processed securely. The Prevention Course is non-refundable after course materials have been accessed. Premium subscriptions can be cancelled at any time.

7. Donations

Donations are voluntary and non-refundable. 100% of donations are used to maintain the platform and fund free counseling sessions. As a student project, we are not a registered charity.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025 · Student Project by Team Return

🔒 Your privacy matters deeply to us. RESET collects minimal data and will never sell your information to third parties. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how you can control it.

1. Who We Are

RESET is a student project by Team Return. We built this platform to support young adults in their recovery journey. We take your privacy seriously, especially given the sensitive nature of our community.

2. What Data We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to operate the platform:

  • Account data: First name, email address, age, password (encrypted)
  • Usage data: Pages visited, features used, session duration (anonymized)
  • Chat data: Messages in public and private chats (stored for service functionality)
  • Payment data: Processed securely via third-party providers — we do not store card details
  • Donation data: Amount donated (no name stored unless you provide one)

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To operate and improve the RESET platform
  • To connect you with counseling sessions and group meetings
  • To send account-related notifications (no marketing emails without consent)
  • To detect and prevent abuse or violations of our community guidelines

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. We may share minimal data with:

  • Payment processors (for transactions only)
  • Hosting providers (to run the platform)
  • Law enforcement, if legally required

5. Counseling Confidentiality

Content shared in 1-on-1 counseling sessions is treated as confidential. Volunteer counselors are bound by our confidentiality guidelines. In cases of immediate risk to life, counselors may be required to involve emergency services.

6. Cookies

We use minimal cookies necessary for the platform to function (e.g. session cookies to keep you logged in). We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

7. Advertisements

RESET displays occasional third-party advertisements to non-Premium users. These ads may use anonymized contextual data but not personal profile data. Premium subscribers see no ads.

8. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and data at any time by contacting us.

9. Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are based in the EU, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Object to processing of your data
  • Data portability

10. Security

We use industry-standard encryption and security practices to protect your data. However, no system is 100% secure — please use a strong, unique password for your RESET account.

11. Minors

RESET is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

12. Contact & Data Requests

For any privacy-related requests, contact us via Instagram @reset.path. We aim to respond within 30 days.
